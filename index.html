Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

The ideal time to visit Andaman is between October and May when the weather is pleasant, the sea is calm, and underwater visibility for scuba diving and snorkeling is at its peak. Water sports remain open and inter-island ferry services operate smoothly.
Indian citizens do not require a passport or special permit to visit Port Blair, Havelock, or Neil Island; a standard government ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) is sufficient. Foreign nationals require a valid Indian Visa. Restricted Area Permits (RAP) are no longer required for 29 inhabited islands for foreigners.
